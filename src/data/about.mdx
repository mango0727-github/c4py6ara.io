Hi, my name is Joonyoung Jeong, but you can just call me Joon. I have been serving as a military officer since 2013, and I am currently a Ph.D. student and a lecturer at the [Korea Military Academy](https://www.kma.ac.kr).

My research interests lie in cellular network security (5G RAN and 5GC) and computer systems security. I am eager to study wireless security because I believe it will only become more critical as everything we carry is increasingly connected through wireless networks — and with such growth in connectivity, there will inevitably be people trying to exploit vulnerabilities in wireless devices to infiltrate otherwise secure systems. I also enjoy dissecting binaries to uncover vulnerabilities and analyze malware functionality.

Outside of work, I am passionate about tennis and horse riding. I feel privileged to have the opportunity to ride at the KMA, as it has its own stable. You can find my rides (and other things that fascinate me) on the [blog](/blog/).
